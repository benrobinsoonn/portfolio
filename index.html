Who I am

Driven by maths,
powered by code

I'm an ASEAN Undergraduate Scholar at the National University of Singapore, studying Computer Science with a second major in Mathematics. My work sits at the intersection of AI research, software engineering, and mathematical problem-solving.

I earned a Bronze Medal at the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) 2024 and the Asian Pacific Mathematics Olympiad (APMO), and was selected as one of Indonesia's Top 9 delegates for the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence 2025.

In 2022, I founded The Strikers Edu — a Mathematics and Science Olympiad training platform that now serves 100+ active students. I believe precision thinking, creativity, and technology together can build a better future.
